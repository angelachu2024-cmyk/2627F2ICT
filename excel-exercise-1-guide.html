03

Calculate Profit/Loss

The percentage is stored as a decimal in Excel, so multiplication gives the money amount directly.

Enter a formula to compute the Profit/ Loss.

𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐟𝐢𝐭 / 𝐋𝐨𝐬𝐬 = 𝐂𝐨𝐬𝐭 𝐏𝐫𝐢𝐜𝐞 × 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐟𝐢𝐭 / 𝐋𝐨𝐬𝐬 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐜𝐞𝐧𝐭

Click D5 and enter:

=B5*C5

Press Enter. This multiplies Blue Pen's cost price by its profit/loss percentage.

What should D5 show?Before currency formatting, the result is 1 because $2 × 50% = $1.
Why drag?Excel adjusts row references automatically: D6 becomes =B6*C6, and so on.

04

Calculate Selling Price

𝐒𝐞𝐥𝐥𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐏𝐫𝐢𝐜𝐞 = 𝐂𝐨𝐬𝐭 𝐏𝐫𝐢𝐜𝐞 + 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐟𝐢𝐭 / 𝐋𝐨𝐬𝐬

Click E5 and enter:

=B5+D5

Press Enter, then drag the fill handle from E5 down to E12.

Quick check: Blue Pen's selling price is 3. Eraser 's selling price is 5.6.
Selling Price filled through row 12?

06

Show money values to two decimals

Select four separate ranges, then use the Home tab's Number controls to show exactly two digits after the decimal.

Select these exact ranges

B5:B12Cost Price
D5:D12Profit/Loss
E5:E12Selling Price
G5:G12Total Profit/Loss
Target: values look like 2.00, 1.00, and -0.99 before the dollar symbol is added.
All four ranges show two decimals?
07

Apply the dollar format

Keep the same four ranges selected and apply the currency button from the Home tab.

Why the order matters: selecting the ranges first lets one formatting action apply consistently to every money column.
Currency applied to all four ranges?
08

Align and finish the worksheet

Use the Home tab's Merge & Center and Alignment controls to match the finished layout.

Tip: A formula can be copied and pasted into other cells with automatically updated values once it is correctly typed. For example, in cell D5, type in =B5*C5. Excel will calculate the total for you. Copy D5 and paste it into D6 and so on, you will notice that excel automatically detected the change in values for you.
