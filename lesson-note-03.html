Lesson 03

Summary Formulas and Sorting Data

This lesson prepares you for the exercise where you calculate totals, averages, maximums, minimums, counts, and filtered results in a sales worksheet.

1. Common Summary Formulas

  • Total adds values together.
  • Average finds the middle value by calculation.
  • Maximum finds the biggest value.
  • Minimum finds the smallest value.
Examples: =SUM(B5:B12), =AVERAGE(B5:B12), =MAX(B5:B12), =MIN(B5:B12)
Use the same idea for several columns by copying formulas across the row.

2. Count, CountIf, and Filters

  • COUNT counts numbers in a range.
  • COUNTIF counts cells that meet one condition.
  • AutoFilter lets you show only selected records.
  • Sort arranges data from largest to smallest or A to Z.
Sorting and filtering idea

Filter: show only rows that match a rule.

Sort: reorder the rows.

Example: sort Total Profit/Loss from largest to smallest.

ItemTotal Profit/Loss

3Glue$3.00
When you sort or filter, always check that your formulas still point to the correct cells.

3. Alignment and Formatting

  • Titles are often bold and centered.
  • Money values are often formatted with a dollar sign and two decimals.
  • Percentages may be aligned to the left to match a model worksheet.
